# Photobooth
1. click the link!: https://shreelakshminarasimhan.github.io/B22Photobooth/
2. to open the photobooth click the camera icon.
3. the trackpad and x box are for 2/ 4 pictures respectively
4. press a or y for colour/ b&w respectively
5. a timer will show, once your pictures are taken press the start button to get your photos!
